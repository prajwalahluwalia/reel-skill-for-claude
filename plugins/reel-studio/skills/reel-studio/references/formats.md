# Reel formats

Each format below has a beat structure. Times assume a 30 to 45 second reel. Stretch or squeeze the middle, never the hook.

## Talking head with b-roll
Creator speaks to camera, cut with supporting clips.
1. Hook (0–2s)
2. Why it matters to the viewer (2–6s)
3. 2–3 points, each with a b-roll cutaway (6–30s)
4. Payoff and CTA (last 5s)

**Best for:** opinions, advice, explainers.

## Tutorial or how-to
Step-by-step with the result shown first.
1. Show the finished result (0–2s)
2. "Here's how" (2–4s)
3. Steps, one per cut, with a step number on screen
4. Final result again and a "save this" CTA

**Best for:** saves. Keep steps to 3–5.

## List or ranking
"X things that…"
1. Hook with the number
2. Items in reverse order of strength, with the best saved for last
3. Quick recap or a question CTA ("which one are you trying?")

**Best for:** shares and saves.

## Storytime
A personal story with a turn.
1. Drop into the most dramatic moment (0–3s)
2. Rewind: "So here's what happened…"
3. Build: 2–3 beats of rising tension
4. Twist or lesson
5. Tie back to the opening moment

**Best for:** follows and comments. Use cuts and angle changes so it isn't one long take.

## Myth vs fact
1. "Myth:" on screen with the common belief
2. Buzzer or cut, then "Fact:" with the truth
3. Quick proof or demo
4. Repeat 2–3 times or end with a CTA

**Best for:** authority and comments.

## Before and after or transformation
1. Before (1–2s)
2. Fast-paced process montage
3. After reveal, timed to the beat drop
4. Text overlay with the key tip

**Best for:** reach. Needs strong visuals and the right audio.

## POV or skit
A relatable scenario acted out, often with the creator playing several characters.
1. "POV:" text with the setup
2. Escalation of the situation
3. Punchline or relatable twist (a loop works well)

**Best for:** shares. Keep it under 20 seconds.

## Day in the life or vlog
1. Hook with the day's twist ("a day as a 19-year-old founder")
2. Fast clips with time stamps on screen
3. One standout moment or lesson
4. End-of-day reflection line

**Best for:** follows and connection.

## Green screen reaction
Creator in front of a screenshot, article, tweet or post.
1. Hook referencing the screenshot
2. Point at the key detail
3. Your take or breakdown
4. Question CTA

**Best for:** news, trends and commentary. Credit the source.

## Faceless text-led
B-roll or aesthetic footage with on-screen text carrying the message, plus voiceover or music.
1. Bold text hook over a moving visual
2. 3–5 text beats, each held about 2 seconds
3. Final takeaway and CTA

**Best for:** creators who don't want to be on camera. Pace the text so it can be read.
