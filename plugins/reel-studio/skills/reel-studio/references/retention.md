# Retention and pacing

Watch time is what gets a reel pushed. These are the levers.

## Pacing
- **Visual change every 2–4 seconds:** a cut, punch-in zoom, new angle, b-roll, prop or text pop-up.
- **About 2.5 spoken words per second.** Faster suits high-energy content. Slower suits calm or expert content.
- **Jump cuts** remove breaths and pauses. Cut tight.
- **Open loops:** tease something early ("the third one surprised me") and pay it off later.

## Structure
- **One idea per reel.** If it needs "and also…", it's two reels.
- **Front-load value.** The first useful thing should arrive by second 5.
- **Payoff before the end.** Don't hold the answer until after the CTA.
- **Loop endings:** write the last line so it leads back into the first. Viewers often watch twice without noticing.

## On-screen text
- Keep each text block short enough to read twice in the time it's on screen.
- Use captions for all speech (many viewers watch muted). Auto-captions are fine, but check them.
- Highlight 1–2 keywords per caption in color or bold, not every word.

## Audio
- Voiceover sits on top, with music at about 10–20% volume.
- Cut on the beat for montages and transformations.
- A sound effect (whoosh, pop, ding) on key text reveals helps attention.

## CTA by goal
| Goal | CTA |
|---|---|
| Saves | "Save this so you have it when…" |
| Shares | "Send this to the friend who…" |
| Comments | Ask a specific, easy question ("A or B?") |
| Follows | "Follow for part 2" (and actually post part 2) |
| Sales | "Link in bio" or "comment X and I'll DM it to you" |
