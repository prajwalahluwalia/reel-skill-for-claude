# Hook types

A hook has one job: make the viewer need the next 3 seconds. Use these as patterns, not fill-in-the-blank scripts. Adapt each one to the creator's voice.

| Type | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Contrarian** | Say the opposite of the common advice | "Stop drinking water first thing in the morning. Here's what to do instead." |
| **Curiosity gap** | Point at something without revealing it | "This one setting is why your photos look flat." |
| **Specific result** | A concrete outcome with numbers | "I saved ₹40,000 in 3 months with one rule." |
| **Mistake callout** | Name a mistake the viewer is likely making | "If your reel gets under 300 views, you're probably doing this." |
| **Relatable POV** | Put the viewer inside a familiar moment | "POV: it's 2am and you're still 'just checking one thing'." |
| **Story drop-in** | Start in the middle of the action | "I got fired on a Monday. By Friday I had three offers." |
| **List promise** | Number of items plus the payoff | "3 apps that do your boring work for you." |
| **Challenge or test** | Try something on camera | "I used only AI to plan my week. Let's see if it works." |
| **Direct address** | Name exactly who this is for | "If you're a student with no money, watch this." |
| **Visual shock** | An unexpected first frame, with no words needed | Pouring coffee into a laptop (that turns out to be a cake) |
| **Before and after** | Show the end result first | Messy desk → spotless desk, then "here's how" |
| **Question** | A question the viewer can't help answering | "Would you take ₹1 crore if you had to delete Instagram forever?" |

## What makes a hook work

- **Visual first.** Many people watch muted. The first frame and the text must work without sound.
- **Specific beats vague.** "3 apps" beats "some apps". "In 7 days" beats "fast".
- **Movement in the first second.** Start mid-gesture, walk in or cut fast. Don't start on a still face that's about to talk.
- **No warm-up.** Cut "Hey guys", "So…" and any intro before the hook.
- **Text and speech say different things.** On-screen text can add context ("day 1 of 30") while the voice says the hook.
- **Keep the promise.** Clickbait that doesn't pay off hurts watch time and trust.
