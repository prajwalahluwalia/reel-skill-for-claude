# Platform specs

Limits change often. Treat these as a starting point and tell the creator to check the app if something matters (for example a reel over 90 seconds).

| | Instagram Reels | TikTok | YouTube Shorts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aspect ratio | 9:16 | 9:16 | 9:16 |
| Resolution | 1080 × 1920 | 1080 × 1920 | 1080 × 1920 |
| Sweet-spot length | 15–45s | 20–60s | 20–50s |
| Max length (check the app) | Several minutes | Up to 10 min in-app | About 3 min |
| Hashtags | 3–5 specific | 3–5 specific | 1–3, or #Shorts |
| Cover | Pick a frame or upload. The grid crops to 3:4 | Pick a frame | Pick a frame |

## Safe zones for text (1080 × 1920)
The app's buttons and caption cover parts of the screen. Keep important text and faces inside:
- **Top:** leave about 250 px clear (account name and tabs)
- **Bottom:** leave about 400 px clear (caption, audio and buttons)
- **Right:** leave about 150 px clear (like, comment and share icons)

In short: keep key text in the middle two thirds of the screen, slightly left of center.

## Cross-posting
- Remove the other platform's watermark before reposting (download the original export, not the in-app save).
- Rewrite the caption for each platform. TikTok captions can be shorter. YouTube titles should be searchable.
